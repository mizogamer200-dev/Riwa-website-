Oil production brings a second stream to the surface: water.

Produced water needs careful management because its composition varies between fields. Reuse is possible only after fit-for-purpose treatment and testing.

THE WATER OPPORTUNITY

What if produced water became a resource?

Oil production can bring substantial volumes of produced water to the surface. When this water is disposed of instead of recovered, it represents a missed opportunity to reduce demand for freshwater in compatible industrial uses. But produced water can contain salts, hydrocarbons and other contaminants: reuse requires suitable treatment, testing and approval.

THE CHALLENGE

Water comes to the surface with oil. What happens next?

Produced water may contain dispersed oil, suspended solids and dissolved substances. It requires appropriate separation, treatment and testing before any intended reuse.
